About me

Curiosity that keeps moving down the stack.

I have been drawn to technology since I was ten, when I began using Ubuntu and writing C and C++ in Code::Blocks. As a teenager, I spent countless hours deploying and troubleshooting Linux systems and low level programming simply to understand how they worked.

I slowed down while studying law, but never lost that curiosity. At the Ceará's State Court, where I worked as a judge’s assistant, I explored the meeting point between legal reasoning and technology. Since 2023, I have returned to computing with renewed focus, following the reading path of USP’s Computer Science curriculum and building across cloud, backend, data, and low-level systems.
